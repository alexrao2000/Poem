Instrument recordings: VSCO 2 Community Edition, Versilian Studios.

orchestra-sources.json lists all 33 selected recordings, source pitches and Git
blob hashes. The renderer mixes them to mono, filters before pitch resampling,
applies note envelopes, then places the original notes in a stereo arrangement.
Percussion and additional synthetic voices/effects are generated locally.
The raw recordings stay in the ignored build cache; the game ships only the
rendered music/effects, this credit and the upstream license. No Nintendo
recordings or melodies are used.

Regenerate with NumPy installed:
python3 scripts/fetch_score_samples.py
python3 scripts/generate_score.py

The score script also accepts --synthetic for its oscillator-only arrangement.
